# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to knows something you do not.

## Why this project needs one specifically

Most of the work here is people describing bureaucracy they have personally been through, often in a
country that was not always welcoming to them, often in their second or third language. That is the
whole value of the dataset and it is also fragile: somebody who gets a condescending reply to their
first pull request does not open a second one.

So, concretely:

- **Correct the data, not the person.** "This changed in 2025, here is the notice" is a contribution.
  "Obviously wrong" is not.
- **Imperfect English is not an error.** The person filing it knows the process. Fix the wording in a
  follow-up commit and move on.
- **Immigration status is not a topic for opinions here.** People's paperwork is the subject matter;
  their right to be somewhere is not up for discussion in this repository.
- **Do not post anybody's documents.** Not yours, not anyone's. Rules are public data; the things
  they describe are not. A pull request containing a real passport number will be removed and its
  history rewritten.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, and personal attacks. Sexual attention or imagery. Publishing anyone's private
information. Sustained disruption. Any of the above in issues, pull requests, commits, or private
messages arising from this project.

## Enforcement

Report to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports stay confidential.

Responses range from a private word, to editing or removing a contribution, to a ban. Enforcement is
by the maintainer, who will recuse where they are involved and ask an uninvolved contributor to
decide.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
